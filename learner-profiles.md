---
title: 'Learner Profiles'
---

This lesson targets ePIC collaboration members — students, postdocs, and researchers — who are
comfortable starting `eic-shell` and want to write or modify reconstruction code in EICrecon. A
typical learner has completed the [Setting Up Your EIC Environment](https://eic.github.io/tutorial-setting-up-environment/)
tutorial, has some basic C++ and Git experience, and now needs to build a plugin or a JANA2 factory
to produce custom histograms or a new reconstruction algorithm.
